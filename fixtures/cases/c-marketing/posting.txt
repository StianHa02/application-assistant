Marketing Internship – Molde Sykkel AS

Molde Sykkel AS sells bikes and equipment in-store and online. We offer a 50% internship in spring 2027 for a student who wants to learn digital marketing.

Responsibilities:
- Create content for Instagram, TikTok and newsletters
- Plan and follow up campaigns
- Analyse results from ads

Qualifications:
- Studies marketing, business or a similar field
- Experience creating content for social media
- Good writing skills in Norwegian
- Experience with Google Ads or Meta advertising
- Can edit video, for example in CapCut or Premiere Pro
- Interest in cycling and outdoor life

It is an advantage if you:
- Have worked with newsletters, for example in Mailchimp
- Have experience from retail or customer service
