Summer Job 2027: IT Support – Nordvik Kraft AS

Nordvik Kraft AS is a regional energy company with 180 employees in Møre og Romsdal. We are looking for two students to join our IT department this summer (weeks 25–32).

Responsibilities:
- User support by phone, chat and in person
- Preparing and rolling out new PCs
- Troubleshooting networks and printers
- Simple scripting to automate routine tasks

We are looking for someone who:
- Studies IT, computer science or a similar field
- Has experience with customer service or user support
- Knows Windows and Microsoft 365
- Has basic knowledge of networking
- Can write simple scripts, for example in PowerShell or Python
- Speaks and writes Norwegian well

Nice to have:
- Experience with Microsoft Intune or Azure
- Knowledge of ITIL
- Driver's licence (class B)

Application deadline: 1 February 2027.
