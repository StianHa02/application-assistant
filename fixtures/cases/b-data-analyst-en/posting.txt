Summer Internship 2027 – Junior Data Analyst
Fjordline Analytics AS, Ålesund

Fjordline Analytics helps shipping and seafood companies make better decisions with data. We are looking for a curious student to join our analytics team for ten weeks next summer.

What you will do:
- Clean and prepare datasets from our customers
- Build reports and dashboards
- Present findings to customers together with a senior analyst

Requirements:
- Ongoing bachelor or master degree in a quantitative field (statistics, economics, logistics, computer science or similar)
- Experience with Excel
- Experience with Python (pandas) for data analysis
- Experience with Power BI or a similar BI tool
- Good communication skills in English
- Ability to explain results to non-technical people

Nice to have:
- SQL
- Knowledge of the shipping or seafood industry
- Experience with cloud data platforms such as Azure or Databricks
